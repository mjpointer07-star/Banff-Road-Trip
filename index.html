🚗 June 19 — Drive to Great Falls, Montana

Colorado → Great Falls, MT (~10–11 hours)

Route: I-25 N through Wyoming → I-90 W → I-15 N → Great Falls, MT

💡 Drive tips:
  • 4 drivers — rotate every 2.5–3 hours
  • Fill up gas in Wyoming (long stretches between stations)
  • Pack food/snacks for the road
  • Download podcasts & playlists — cell service gets spotty in Montana

🏨 Overnight: Great Falls, MT

⛰️ June 20 — Arrive at Glacier NP & Explore

Great Falls → Glacier NP (~2.5 hrs) + Full Day Exploring

Drive from Great Falls to Glacier's West Entrance: ~2.5 hours. Arrive by mid-morning if you leave early.

Must-Do Today: Going-to-the-Sun Road

  • Drive Going-to-the-Sun Road — 50 miles of jaw-dropping mountain scenery
  • Logan Pass (6,646 ft) — Hike Hidden Lake Overlook (3mi RT, boardwalk, mountain goats)
  • Stop at pullouts for Bird Woman Falls & Weeping Wall

Afternoon

  • Trail of the Cedars — Short boardwalk through ancient cedar forest
  • Avalanche Lake (4.5mi RT, moderate) — Glacier-fed lake surrounded by waterfalls

Evening

  • Dinner in West Glacier or Apgar Village
  • Sunset at Lake McDonald — mirror-calm water, colorful rocks on the shore

🏨 Overnight: Glacier NP area (West Glacier / Apgar)

⛰️ June 21 — Full Day at Glacier National Park

Many Glacier — The "Switzerland of North America"

Morning (start early — 7am)

  • Drive to Many Glacier area (~1.5 hrs from West Glacier)
  • Hike Grinnell Glacier (10.6mi RT, strenuous) — Turquoise lakes, active glacier, wildflowers
  • Shorter option: Iceberg Lake (9.6mi RT, moderate) — Icebergs floating in the lake even in summer
  • Easy option: Redrock Falls (3.6mi RT)

Afternoon

  • Boat tour on Swiftcurrent Lake & Lake Josephine (~1.5 hrs, scenic, narrated)
  • Watch for grizzlies, moose, mountain goats — this is prime wildlife territory

Alternative: East Side of Going-to-the-Sun Road

  • St. Mary Falls + Virginia Falls (3.6mi RT) — Two beautiful waterfalls
  • Sun Point — Easy lakeside walk with mountain views
  • Highline Trail from Logan Pass (11.8mi point-to-point) — Cliff-edge trail along the Continental Divide

🏨 Overnight: Glacier NP area

Glacier NP — Key Info:
  • NO vehicle reservation required in 2026 — just drive in!
  • Entrance: $35/vehicle (7-day) or America the Beautiful Pass ($80/year)
  • No Logan Pass parking time limit before July 1
  • Bring microspikes for snowy high-elevation trails
  • Going-to-the-Sun Road typically fully opens June 16–25. Check: nps.gov/glac/planyourvisit/roadstatus.htm

🛣️ June 22 — Glacier → Waterton → Banff

Morning: Waterton Lakes National Park

Cross the border at Chief Mountain (seasonal, confirm open) or Carway/Piegan. Waterton is right across — Glacier's Canadian sister park.

  • Cameron Falls — 2-minute walk from town, vivid blue-green water
  • Red Rock Canyon — Beautiful red rock gorge, easy walking trails
  • Prince of Wales Hotel viewpoint — Iconic hilltop vista over the lake
  • Waterton Shoreline Cruise — Boat ride down Upper Waterton Lake
  • Grab lunch in Waterton townsite

FREE ADMISSION starting June 19, 2026

Afternoon Drive: Waterton → Banff (~3–4 hrs)

Stops Along the Way (optional):

  • Head-Smashed-In Buffalo Jump (UNESCO) — Indigenous bison jump site, excellent interpretive centre (1–2 hrs)
  • Frank Slide (Crowsnest Pass) — 1903 rockslide that buried a town, eerie debris field (1 hr)

Arrive in Banff/Canmore by evening. Settle in, grab dinner.

🍁 June 23–25 — Banff & Area

FREE PARKS CANADA ADMISSION June 19 – Sept 7, 2026

June 23 — ✅ RESERVED: Lake Louise, Moraine Lake & Castle Mountain Shuttle Tour

Early Morning

  • Catch shuttle (earliest departure for best light — before 10am)
  • Moraine Lake — Rockpile Trail for the classic viewpoint, walk the lakeshore
  • Take connector shuttle to Lake Louise

Late Morning / Afternoon

  • Lake Louise — Walk to back of lake (Victoria Glacier views)
  • Optional: Hike to Lake Agnes Tea House (7km RT) or canoe on the lake
  • Castle Mountain drive-by on Bow Valley Parkway (Hwy 1A)
📸 Photography tip: Morning light is best — clouds build after 2pm. Get your shots of both lakes early!

🏨 Overnight: Banff

June 24 — Banff Day (Icefields Parkway or Town Activities)

Option A: Icefields Parkway Day Trip (Recommended)

Highway 93N — one of the most beautiful drives in the world. 230km of glaciers, turquoise lakes, waterfalls.

  • Bow Lake — Turquoise lake right off the highway
  • Peyto Lake — Wolf-head-shaped lake, impossibly blue
  • Mistaya Canyon — Narrow slot canyon with churning water
  • Columbia Icefield — Ice Explorer Tour onto the glacier + Glacier Skywalk

Option B: Banff Town Day

  • Banff Gondola up Sulphur Mountain — 360° panoramic views
  • Bow Falls — Easy walk from downtown
  • Banff Upper Hot Springs — Soak with mountain views
  • Johnston Canyon — Lower Falls, Upper Falls, Ink Pots

Option C: Adventure Day

  • Kicking Horse River rafting — Class III–IV rapids (half day)
  • Kananaskis — Grassi Lakes (4km RT) — Turquoise pools, cliff views
  • Lake Minnewanka boat cruise + Two Jack Lake
  • Legacy Trail — Bike between Banff and Canmore (22km, flat)

🏨 Overnight: Banff

June 25 — Banff Morning → Drive to West Yellowstone

Morning in Banff (pick one before you leave):

  • Johnston Canyon — Lower Falls + Upper Falls (2.7km, ~1.5 hrs). Do this if you didn't on June 24.
  • Banff Gondola — If you haven't done it yet
  • Vermilion Lakes — Quick canoe/kayak paddle, Mt. Rundle views
  • Banff Upper Hot Springs — Relaxing soak before a long drive
  • Browse Banff Avenue shops, grab a coffee

Afternoon: Drive to West Yellowstone (~8–9 hrs)

Route: Banff → Calgary → Hwy 2 south → Sweetgrass border crossing → I-15 south → Bozeman → West Yellowstone

💡 Drive strategy: Leave Banff by noon, arrive West Yellowstone by 8–9pm. 4 drivers makes this manageable. Fill up gas before crossing the border — cheaper in Canada with the exchange rate sometimes.

🦬 June 26–28 — Yellowstone National Park

With nearly 3 days in Yellowstone, you can see everything without rushing. The park is huge — think of it in zones.

June 26 — Geyser Country & Grand Canyon of Yellowstone

Morning: Geyser Basins (enter West Entrance)

  • Grand Prismatic Spring — Giant rainbow hot spring (largest in North America)
  • Grand Prismatic Overlook Trail (1.6mi RT) — Aerial view, worth the short hike
  • Midway Geyser Basin boardwalk
  • Old Faithful — Watch the eruption (every ~90 min, check predicted time at visitor center)
  • Walk the Upper Geyser Basin loop — Morning Glory Pool, Castle Geyser, Riverside Geyser

Afternoon: Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone

  • Artist Point — The classic viewpoint of Lower Falls (308 ft waterfall, golden canyon walls)
  • Uncle Tom's Trail — Steep staircase descent for a closer view (328 steps down and back up)
  • Upper Falls viewpoint — Different angle, quick stop
  • Drive through Hayden Valley — Watch for bison herds, bears, wolves

Evening

  • Return to West Yellowstone for dinner
  • Check eruption predictions for next day if you want to catch other geysers

🏨 Overnight: West Yellowstone, MT

June 27 — Lamar Valley, Mammoth Hot Springs & Northern Loop

Early Morning (6–8am): Lamar Valley Wildlife

  • Drive to Lamar Valley — The "Serengeti of North America"
  • Best wildlife viewing at dawn: bison herds, wolves, grizzlies, pronghorn, coyotes
  • Bring binoculars & a thermos of coffee — pull over and watch
  • This is your best chance to see wolves in the wild

Mid-Morning: Mammoth Hot Springs

  • Mammoth Hot Springs terraces — Steaming travertine limestone formations
  • Walk upper and lower terrace boardwalks (~1.5 hrs total)
  • Elk often graze right on the lawns here

Afternoon: Options

  • Tower Fall — Quick viewpoint stop on the way back
  • Yellowstone Lake — Drive the east side, walk the shore
  • Mud Volcano area — Bubbling mud pots, Dragon's Mouth Spring
  • Or revisit any geyser basin — evening light at Grand Prismatic is different than morning

🏨 Overnight: West Yellowstone, MT

June 28 — Yellowstone Morning → Grand Teton → Drive Home

Morning: Last Yellowstone Stops

  • West Thumb Geyser Basin — Hot springs bubbling into Yellowstone Lake (quick 30 min boardwalk)
  • Any spots you missed or want to revisit
  • Exit via South Entrance into Grand Teton

Grand Teton Quick Stops (on the way south):

  • Oxbow Bend — Classic Teton reflection, often moose or pelicans
  • Signal Mountain Summit Drive — Short drive up for panoramic Teton views
  • Jenny Lake — Quick stop at a turquoise alpine lake (optional)
  • Jackson, WY — Grab lunch, see the elk antler arches in town square

Afternoon/Evening: Drive Home

  • Route: Jackson → Rock Springs, WY → I-80 east → I-25 south → Colorado
  • ~7–8 hours from Jackson to Colorado
  • 4 drivers, rotate and push through

🏠 HOME by late evening

Yellowstone — Key Info:
  • No reservation needed — just entrance pass ($35/vehicle or America the Beautiful)
  • Bison jams: Herds walk on the road. Can add 15–30+ min delays. Can't avoid it — enjoy it!
  • Gas: Fill up in West Yellowstone. Also available at Old Faithful, Canyon, Grant Village.
  • Food: Pack lunches/snacks — park food is mediocre and overpriced
  • Cell service: Basically none inside the park. Download offline maps.
  • Start early: Popular parking lots (Old Faithful, Grand Prismatic, Lamar) fill by 10am in summer.
