⚠️ Read first — three things that shape the trip

  • Going-to-the-Sun Road won't be fully open over Logan Pass (projected ~July 1). East side is fully open — Many Glacier, Two Medicine, St. Mary are the best scenery. No vehicle reservations needed in 2026.
  • Lake Louise & Moraine Lake are covered by your June 23 bus tour. No separate shuttle to book — the tour handles access.
  • Passports for everyone. Chief Mountain border: 7am–10pm in June.
  • ☀️ Daylight: Sunrise ~5:30am, sunset ~9:30pm at all locations. 16 hrs of light — start hikes early, enjoy long evenings.

🚨 Emergency contacts

  • Glacier NP Rangers: (406) 888-7800
  • Banff Emergency: 911 (or Parks Canada dispatch: 403-762-1470)
  • Yellowstone Rangers: (307) 344-7381
  • Roadside Assistance (AAA): 1-800-222-4357
  • Nearest hospitals: Kalispell Regional (Glacier) · Canmore Hospital (Banff) · Livingston HealthCare (Yellowstone)

Day 3 · SundayJun 21 — Glacier Day 2: Full Day Park▸

Pick your adventure

  • St. Mary Lake + GTSR east — Sun Point, St. Mary & Virginia Falls, Jackson Glacier Overlook
  • Two Medicine — quieter, Running Eagle Falls, boat tour

Leg two · Jun 22–24
Across the border — Waterton & Banff
Passports out · turquoise lakes, the booked tour, and the Icefields Parkway
Day 4 · MondayJun 22 — Glacier → Waterton → Banff Drive▸

Morning: Waterton Lakes NP

  • Chief Mountain border — passports ready!
  • Cameron Falls, Red Rock Canyon, Prince of Wales Hotel viewpoint
  • Lunch in Waterton townsite

FREE — Canada Strong Pass

Afternoon: drive to Banff (~3–4 hrs)

  • Optional: Head-Smashed-In Buffalo Jump, Frank Slide
  • Arrive Banff/Canmore evening

🏨 Banff / Canmore

🍽️ Dinner: Magpie & Stump (Tex-Mex, patio, $12-18) · The Balkan (Greek, live music, $$) · Nourish Bistro (vegetarian-friendly, $$)

Day 5 · Tuesday · Booked ✅Jun 23 — Bus Tour: Lake Louise + Moraine Lake Booked▸
Tour handles all access. No separate shuttle needed. Confirm pickup point & time.
  • Moraine Lake — Rockpile Trail for the classic view
  • Lake Louise — shoreline walk, Victoria Glacier
  • Castle Mountain — drive-by on Bow Valley Parkway

🏨 Banff / Canmore

Day 6 · WednesdayJun 24 — Icefields Parkway Park▸

One of the world's most scenic drives — 230 km of glaciers, turquoise lakes, waterfalls.

  • Bow Lake — turquoise, right off the highway
  • Peyto Lake — wolf-head shape, impossibly blue
  • Mistaya Canyon — slot canyon
  • Columbia Icefield — Ice Explorer + Skywalk (optional, book ahead)
Family pick: Bow Lake + Peyto turnaround hits the icons without exhausting everyone before tomorrow's long drive.

🏨 Banff / Canmore (last Canada night)

⛽ Fill up before the Icefields Parkway — no gas for 150 km!

🍽️ Dinner: Park Distillery (campfire-style, $$) · Farm & Fire (pizza, $15-20) · Grizzly Paw Brewing, Canmore (brewpub, $)

Leg three · Jun 25–28
South to Yellowstone, then home
Geysers, bison jams, the Tetons — and the victory lap to Denver
Day 7 · ThursdayJun 25 — Banff → Gardiner, MT Drive▸

~550 mi · ~9 hrs south through Montana via Bozeman. Border at Sweetgrass/Coutts.

  • If daylight remains: drive into Yellowstone for Mammoth Hot Springs terraces
  • Alternative: break in Bozeman/Livingston overnight

🏨 Gardiner, MT (North Entrance)

⛽ Fill up in Gardiner — limited gas inside Yellowstone!

🍽️ Dinner: K-Bar Pizza (local favorite, $12-18) · Yellowstone Mine (burgers/bison, $$)

Day 8 · FridayJun 26 — Yellowstone Day 1: Wildlife + Canyon Park▸

Dawn: Lamar Valley (6–8am)

  • "Serengeti of North America" — bison, wolves, grizzlies
  • Bring binoculars & coffee, pull over and watch

Mid-morning: Mammoth Hot Springs

  • Steaming travertine terraces, elk on the lawns

Afternoon: Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone

  • Artist Point — Lower Falls (308 ft)
  • Hayden Valley — more bison, possible bears

🏨 Gardiner, MT

Day 9 · SaturdayJun 27 — Geysers → Teton → Jackson Park▸

Morning: geyser basins (south through park)

  • Grand Prismatic Spring + overlook trail
  • Old Faithful — time it with an eruption
  • West Thumb — hot springs on Yellowstone Lake

Afternoon: Grand Teton

  • Exit south → Jackson Lake, Jenny Lake
  • Continue to Jackson, WY

🏨 Jackson, WY

🍽️ Dinner: Liberty Burger (gourmet burgers, $14-18) · Bin22 (wine bar + tapas, $$) · Million Dollar Cowboy Bar (classic western, burgers & atmosphere)
