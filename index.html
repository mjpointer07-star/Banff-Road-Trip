⚠️ Read First — Three Things That Shape the Trip

  • Going-to-the-Sun Road will NOT be fully open over Logan Pass (projected ~July 1). You're on Glacier's east side — Many Glacier, Two Medicine, St. Mary are all open and are the best scenery in late June. No vehicle reservations required in 2026.
  • Lake Louise & Moraine Lake are covered by your June 23 bus tour. The tour handles Moraine access — no separate shuttle to book. Just confirm the pickup point & time.
  • Passports for everyone — non-negotiable at Chief Mountain border. Crossing open 7am–10pm in June.

🗓️ Day-by-Day Itinerary

Jun 19 (Fri) — Denver → Great Falls, MT · ~750 mi / ~12 hr

The Big Highway Push

  • Leave early (4–5am), knock out miles north through Wyoming & Montana
  • 4 drivers rotating every 3 hours — everyone shares the load
  • Fill up gas in Wyoming — long stretches between stations
  • Arrive Great Falls by evening, grab dinner, crash

Jun 20 (Sat) — Great Falls → Glacier NP (East Side) · Glacier Day 1

Morning: Drive to Glacier (~2.5 hrs)

Arrive St. Mary by ~11am, fresh and ready.

Afternoon: Many Glacier

  • Many Glacier — Crown jewel of the east side
  • Swiftcurrent Lake, historic hotel, easy lakeshore strolls
  • Grinnell Lake trail or Redrock Falls (3.6mi RT)
  • Watch for grizzlies, moose, mountain goats
Pass: Show your America the Beautiful pass ($80) — covers Glacier + Yellowstone.

Jun 21 (Sun) — Full Day at Glacier National Park · Glacier Day 2

Options (pick based on energy):

  • St. Mary Lake & GTSR east side — Drive as far as it's plowed: Sun Point, St. Mary & Virginia Falls, Jackson Glacier Overlook
  • Two Medicine — Quieter, stunning. Running Eagle Falls, boat tour
  • Deeper Many Glacier hike — Grinnell Glacier (10.6mi, strenuous) or Iceberg Lake (9.6mi)
  • Boat tour on Swiftcurrent Lake (~1.5 hrs)

🏨 St. Mary / Babb area

Jun 22 (Mon) — Glacier → Waterton → Banff · ~290 mi / ~6 hr

Morning: Waterton Lakes National Park

  • Cross at Chief Mountain border (~1.5 hrs from St. Mary). Passports ready!
  • Cameron Falls — 2-min walk, vivid blue-green water
  • Red Rock Canyon — Easy walking trails in red rock gorge
  • Prince of Wales Hotel viewpoint — Iconic vista
  • Lunch in Waterton townsite

FREE ADMISSION (Canada Strong Pass)

Afternoon: Drive to Banff (~3-4 hrs)

  • Optional stop: Head-Smashed-In Buffalo Jump (UNESCO) — 1-2 hrs
  • Optional stop: Frank Slide — Eerie 1903 rockslide debris (~1 hr)
  • Arrive Banff/Canmore by evening

Jun 23 (Tue) — ✅ Bus Tour: Lake Louise, Moraine Lake & Castle Mountain

Pre-Booked Tour Day

  • The tour handles all Moraine Lake access — no separate shuttle needed
  • Moraine Lake — Rockpile Trail for the classic viewpoint

Evening

🏨 Banff / Canmore

Jun 24 (Wed) — Icefields Parkway Day Trip

One of the World's Most Scenic Roads

Highway 93N from Lake Louise — 230km of glaciers, turquoise lakes, waterfalls.

  • Bow Lake — Turquoise, right off the highway
  • Peyto Lake — Wolf-head shape, impossibly blue
  • Mistaya Canyon — Narrow slot canyon
  • Columbia Icefield — Ice Explorer Tour + Glacier Skywalk (book ahead)
Family pick: Bow Lake + Peyto Lake turnaround (~1 hr north of Lake Louise) hits the icons without wearing everyone out before tomorrow's long drive.

🏨 Banff / Canmore (last Canada night)

Jun 25 (Thu) — Banff → Gardiner, MT (North Yellowstone) · ~550 mi / ~9 hr

Drive Day — Back into the USA

  • ~9 hrs south through Montana via Bozeman
  • Cross back at Sweetgrass/Coutts border (passports)
  • If there's daylight when you arrive, drive up to Mammoth Hot Springs terraces — just inside the North Entrance gate
💡 Softer version: Overnight in Bozeman or Livingston to break the drive, then push to Gardiner early June 26.

🏨 Gardiner, MT (North Entrance)

🗺️ Route Map

📍 Open in Google Maps

Jun 26 (Fri) — Yellowstone Day 1: Lamar Valley + Canyon

Early Morning (6–8am): Lamar Valley

  • The "Serengeti of North America" — best wildlife at dawn
  • Bison herds, wolves, grizzlies, pronghorn, coyotes
  • Bring binoculars & coffee — pull over and watch

Mid-Morning: Mammoth Hot Springs

  • Steaming travertine limestone terraces
  • Upper and lower boardwalks (~1.5 hrs)
  • Elk graze right on the lawns

Afternoon: Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone

  • Artist Point — Classic Lower Falls viewpoint (308 ft waterfall)
  • Uncle Tom's Trail — Steep staircase for closer view
  • Hayden Valley — Drive slow, watch for bison and bears

🏨 Gardiner, MT

Jun 27 (Sat) — Yellowstone Day 2: Geysers → Grand Teton → Jackson

Morning: South Through the Geyser Basins

  • Grand Prismatic Spring — Giant rainbow hot spring + overlook trail
  • Old Faithful — Time it with the eruption (~every 90 min)
  • West Thumb Geyser Basin — Hot springs on Yellowstone Lake

Afternoon: Grand Teton National Park

  • Exit Yellowstone south into Grand Teton (covered by same pass)
  • Jackson Lake — Mountain views
  • Jenny Lake — Quick stop at turquoise alpine lake
  • Continue to Jackson, WY for dinner & overnight
