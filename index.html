⚠️ Read First — Three Things That Shape the Trip

  • Going-to-the-Sun Road won't be fully open over Logan Pass (projected ~July 1). East side is fully open — Many Glacier, Two Medicine, St. Mary are the best scenery. No vehicle reservations needed in 2026.
  • Lake Louise & Moraine Lake covered by your June 23 bus tour. No separate shuttle to book — the tour handles access.
  • Passports for everyone. Chief Mountain border: 7am–10pm in June.

🚨 Emergency Contacts

🏨 St. Mary / Babb

Jun 22 (Mon) — Glacier → Waterton → Banff Drive

Morning: Waterton Lakes NP

  • Chief Mountain border — passports ready!
  • Cameron Falls, Red Rock Canyon, Prince of Wales Hotel viewpoint
  • Lunch in Waterton townsite

FREE — Canada Strong Pass

Afternoon: Drive to Banff (~3–4 hrs)

  • Optional: Head-Smashed-In Buffalo Jump, Frank Slide
  • Arrive Banff/Canmore evening

Jun 23 (Tue) — ✅ Bus Tour: Lake Louise + Moraine Lake Booked
Tour handles all access. No separate shuttle needed. Confirm pickup point & time.
  • Moraine Lake — Rockpile Trail for the classic view
  • Lake Louise — Shoreline walk, Victoria Glacier
  • Castle Mountain — Drive-by on Bow Valley Parkway

🏨 Banff / Canmore

Jun 24 (Wed) — Icefields Parkway Park

One of the world's most scenic drives — 230km of glaciers, turquoise lakes, waterfalls.

  • Bow Lake — Turquoise, right off highway
  • Peyto Lake — Wolf-head shape, impossibly blue
  • Mistaya Canyon — Slot canyon
  • Columbia Icefield — Ice Explorer + Skywalk (optional, book ahead)
Family pick: Bow Lake + Peyto turnaround hits the icons without exhausting everyone before tomorrow's long drive.

🏨 Banff / Canmore (last Canada night)

Jun 25 (Thu) — Banff → Gardiner, MT Drive

~550 mi · ~9 hrs south through Montana via Bozeman. Border at Sweetgrass/Coutts.

  • If daylight remains: drive into Yellowstone for Mammoth Hot Springs terraces
  • Alternative: break in Bozeman/Livingston overnight

Jun 26 (Fri) — Yellowstone Day 1: Wildlife + Canyon Park

Dawn: Lamar Valley (6–8am)

  • "Serengeti of North America" — bison, wolves, grizzlies
  • Bring binoculars & coffee, pull over and watch

Mid-Morning: Mammoth Hot Springs

  • Steaming travertine terraces, elk on the lawns

Afternoon: Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone

  • Artist Point — Lower Falls (308 ft)
  • Hayden Valley — more bison, possible bears

🏨 Gardiner, MT

Jun 27 (Sat) — Yellowstone Day 2: Geysers → Teton → Jackson Park

Morning: Geyser Basins (south through park)

  • Grand Prismatic Spring + overlook trail
  • Old Faithful — time it with eruption
  • West Thumb — hot springs on Yellowstone Lake

Afternoon: Grand Teton

  • Exit south → Jackson Lake, Jenny Lake
  • Continue to Jackson, WY
